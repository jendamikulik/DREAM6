\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This note isolates a single recurring pattern---the \emph{witness/converse bracket}---that appeared independently in two structurally unrelated problems worked on in the same period: a safe-anchor / renormalization program (WPB) and a causal Shannon-floor resource-accounting program (Bill). The pattern is not new. It is the ordinary shape of weak duality, stated abstractly enough to make an otherwise easy-to-miss cross-instantiation visible. The claim is narrow and checkable: WPB's compressed Farkas margin bracket and Bill's synchronous-rate bracket are the same schema with different data substituted in. The shared schema is a \emph{tool}, not a shared \emph{object}. The two problems do not become the same problem by sharing it.
\end{abstract}

\section{What this note is, and is not}

Nothing below is a theorem about an invariant tube, a breathing mode, or a fiber coordinate. Those objects belong to WPB and remain in their own ledger. The present text extracts only the pricing device used to speak honestly about an unknown extremal value.

\section{The abstract mechanism}

Let \(\Acal\) be a feasible set, \(f:\Acal\to\mathbb{R}\) an objective, and
\begin{equation}
  V^\star \;:=\; \operatorname*{ext}_{\pi\in\Acal} f(\pi)
\end{equation}
an unknown extremal quantity (\(\operatorname{ext}\) means \(\inf\) or \(\sup\)), not directly computable because \(\Acal\) or \(f\) is too large or too implicit to optimize exhaustively.

A bracket \([L,U]\ni V^\star\) is built from two logically distinct kinds of certificate. It matters which kind is used on which side.

\paragraph{Species 1 --- converse (relaxation) bounds.}
A bound is a converse if it is established by an argument valid \emph{simultaneously} for every \(\pi\in\Acal\), without exhibiting any particular one. Typical sources: a coordinatewise relaxation of \(f\) or \(\Acal\), weak LP duality, or an information-theoretic inequality (a transport converse, a counting bound). Because the argument covers the whole feasible set at once, it bounds \(V^\star\) for free.

\paragraph{Species 2 --- constructive witnesses.}
A bound is a witness if it is established by exhibiting one explicit \(\pi_0\in\Acal\), verifying admissibility directly (normalization, marginals, positivity, exactness of the induced law), and evaluating \(f(\pi_0)\). This gives \(V^\star\le f(\pi_0)\) (or \(\ge\), for a supremum) trivially, but only because that one object was actually checked.

A bracket is then
\begin{equation}
  L \;\le\; V^\star \;\le\; U,
\end{equation}
where each of \(L,U\) may be built from either species. Both ends can be converses, or one end a converse and the other a witness. The width
\begin{equation}
  \Delta \;:=\; U-L \;\ge\; 0
\end{equation}
is not a hedge. It is an exact, computable statement of present ignorance, and it is falsifiable: any tightening of either side is a checkable claim, not a change of opinion.

This is weak duality plus feasibility. Its only content here is that stating it at this level of abstraction makes two otherwise unrelated computations visibly the same move.

\section{Two instances from the same period}

\subsection{Instance A --- the compressed Farkas margin (WPB)}

Here \(V^\star=\delta_m(y)\), the reduced safety margin of a target point relative to a renormalization-step reachable region \(F_m(\Pcal_m)\subset\mathbb{R}^3\), defined by
\begin{equation}
  \delta_m(y)
  \;=\;
  \inf_{\|z\|_1=1}\bigl(h_m(z)-z\cdot y\bigr),
  \qquad
  h_m(z)
  \;=\;
  \sup_{\pi\in\Pcal_m} z\cdot F_m\pi.
\end{equation}
Direct computation of \(h_m(z)\) becomes expensive as the support of the transport polytope grows. The bracket is obtained by replacing the fine-grained per-cell coefficients of the objective with their coordinatewise minimum (respectively maximum) over coarsened index groups:
\begin{equation}
  h_m^{\mathrm{lower}}(z)
  \;\le\;
  h_m(z)
  \;\le\;
  h_m^{\mathrm{upper}}(z),
\end{equation}
because the supremum of a pointwise-smaller (respectively larger) function over the \emph{same} feasible set is itself smaller (respectively larger). Both ends are Species~1: no explicit coupling is exhibited on either side; both bounds come from the same relaxation device run in opposite directions. Propagated through the margin formula this gives a certified bracket
\begin{equation}
  \delta_{\mathrm{lower}}
  \;\le\;
  \delta_{\mathrm{true}}
  \;\le\;
  \delta_{\mathrm{upper}}.
\end{equation}
On audited states the observed width was of order \(10^{-11}\). That figure is an audit datum, not a property of the schema. The relaxation is structurally approximate and, on those states, numerically decisive.

\subsection{Instance B --- the synchronous rate (Bill)}

Here \(V^\star=C^{\mathrm{syn}}_\infty(P,Q)\), the asymptotic per-symbol entropy required by a synchronous stable-event realization contract for a two-action controlled channel.

The lower bound is Species~1: an information-spectrum / optimal-transport converse. Any synchronous table, evaluated along any admissible history, must dominate a quantile-transport functional of the two actions' surprisal laws; averaging yields a bound of the form
\begin{equation}
  C^{\mathrm{syn}}_\infty
  \;\ge\;
  \tfrac12\bigl(H(P)+H(Q)+W_1(\mu_P,\mu_Q)\bigr),
\end{equation}
valid for every feasible table without exhibiting one.

The upper bound is Species~2: an explicit finite-atom joint law, verified to sum to one, to reproduce the required product marginals under all action pairs, and to have a computable entropy. This is a genuine constructive witness, not a relaxation.

The resulting numerical bracket in the source material mixes both species by construction: converse below, witness above. The gap is posed as an open problem, not smoothed over. The specific numerical endpoints belong to the Bill ledger and are not re-certified here.

\section{The degenerate case: when the bracket closes}

The same period produced one instance where \(L\) and \(U\) were shown to coincide exactly: the causal-tree rate
\begin{equation}
  C^{\mathrm{causal}}_\infty(P,Q)
  \;=\;
  \max\bigl\{H(P),H(Q)\bigr\}.
\end{equation}
The lower bound is immediate Species~1 (any policy that always plays the entropy-maximizing action already forces this rate on the realized trajectory). The upper bound is Species~2 of an asymptotic-family kind: an explicit sequence of finite constructions whose cost converges to the same value as the block length grows.

When a converse and a constructed achieving sequence meet, the bracket does not merely narrow. It stops being a bracket and becomes a theorem. This is the same event, structurally, as a linear program's primal and dual optima coinciding: strong duality is the special case of a bracket of width zero.

Closing a bracket to zero is \emph{not} guaranteed by having a converse and a witness in hand. Both instances in the previous section remain open precisely because no such matching construction has yet been found for them.

\section{What does not transfer}

The shared mechanism must not be mistaken for a shared object. The disanalogy is exact.

Instance~B's bracket certifies one fixed number, attached to one channel, at one asymptotic horizon. There is no second scale at which \(C^{\mathrm{syn}}_\infty\) must be re-established.

Instance~A's bracket certifies one term of a sequence indexed by the renormalization scale \(m\). The open problem is not the value of any single \(\delta_m\)---several individual margins have in fact been bracketed tightly---but whether an admissible lift can be chosen at every \(m\) so that the next scale's bracket is again satisfiable, indefinitely:
\begin{equation}
  \Kfrak_m \;\to\; \Kfrak_{2m}
  \qquad\text{for all }m,
\end{equation}
not merely for one \(m\). This is a viability / invariance question superimposed on the bracketing tool. Nothing in the abstract mechanism addresses it.

A bracket can be computed at every scale individually and the sequence of brackets can still fail to propagate. Conversely, propagation would not by itself make individual brackets tighter.

In the vocabulary of the abstract mechanism: both problems use the same method for pricing an unknown extremal quantity. Only one of them additionally requires that quantity to be re-priced, favourably, forever.

\paragraph{Objects that do not enter this note.}
The WPB shape residual \(\|R_{\mathrm{shape}}\|_F/\|J_{\mathrm{raw}}\|_F\), the fact that \(R_{\mathrm{shape}}\) has codomain in a five-dimensional orthogonal complement but rank at most three, and the later observation that on one MaxEnt section the shape quotient is nearly rank-one and radial, are WPB geometric findings. They are not part of the bracket schema and must not be rewritten as a ``five-dimensional obstruction'' of the present mechanism.

\section{A working checklist}

For an unresolved bracket in either program, or a new one, the following order matches the order in which both instances above were actually built.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What are \(\Acal\) and \(f\), precisely? Is the extremal quantity even well-defined as stated?
  \item Is there a Species-1 converse available cheaply (a relaxation, a duality argument, an information-theoretic inequality) that holds for the whole feasible set at once?
  \item Is there a Species-2 witness available---one explicit, fully verified feasible object, or an explicit sequence of such objects with a provable limiting cost?
  \item Report \(L\), \(U\), and \(\Delta=U-L\) together; never \(U\) or \(L\) alone; never a point estimate presented as if it were either.
  \item Ask separately whether anything about the underlying problem forces this certification to be repeated at a growing family of scales. If so, treat that as a second, independent obligation, not as something the bracket itself discharges.
\end{enumerate}

\section{On priority}

Nothing in the abstract mechanism is new mathematics. It is weak duality together with the standard achievability / converse pairing used throughout information theory and combinatorial optimization, named here only to make an implicit repetition explicit. The contribution of this note is narrow: an explicit check that two concretely different computations, produced independently, are the same schema with different data, and an equally explicit statement of where that shared schema stops mattering.

\section*{One sentence}

Two problems shared a way of pricing what they did not yet know; they did not, on that account, share what they were trying to price.
